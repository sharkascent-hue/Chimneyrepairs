Chimney Repairs & Rebuilds looks after chimneys for homeowners and businesses across Dublin and the surrounding areas. Chimney stacks take the worst of the weather, so mortar crumbles, bricks loosen and flashing lifts. We put that right before it becomes a bigger, dearer problem.

We assess every chimney before recommending anything, match the repair to the property, and keep you informed from the first visit to the final clean-up.
