Chimney work we carry out.

A complete range of domestic and commercial chimney work across Dublin.

01

Chimney Stack Repairs

Rain, wind and frost slowly open up mortar joints, loosen bricks and weaken sections of masonry. Left alone, a small defect becomes a structural one.

We assess the stack first, then repair only what needs it, keeping the character of the property, whether it is a terrace, a period house or a modern home.

  • Loose or damaged bricks
  • Cracked or missing mortar
  • Loose chimney pots
  • Weather-damaged masonry

02

Chimney Stack Rebuilding

When a stack is leaning, badly cracked or too far gone to repair, rebuilding is the safe, lasting answer.

We take the stack down to sound brickwork, rebuild it with quality materials, then finish with new flaunching, pots and flashing so it is weathertight from day one.

  • Leaning or unstable stacks
  • Partial and full rebuilds
  • New pots and flaunching
  • Scaffold and safe access
Get a free quote

03

Chimney Flashing

Flashing seals the junction where the chimney meets the roof. When it cracks, lifts or was badly fitted, water gets into the roof space and shows up as damp patches and stained ceilings.

We repair or replace flashing and re-fix it properly into the masonry. It is a common fault on older Dublin houses, and worth checking before it damages timbers.

  • Cracked or split flashing
  • Loose or lifted flashing
  • Failed mortar fixings
  • Full replacement
Get a free quote

04

Chimney Mortar Repair

Mortar is the first thing to fail on an exposed chimney. Once joints open up, water gets into the masonry and frost does the rest.

We cut out the failed mortar and renew it with a suitable mix, restoring the strength of the stack and stopping water penetration.

  • Crumbling joints
  • Water penetration
  • Frost damage
  • Matching existing mortar
Get a free quote

05

Chimney Repointing

Repointing replaces worn mortar joints across the whole stack, improving both its strength and its appearance.

It is one of the most cost-effective ways to extend the life of a chimney and prevent water damage inside the house.

  • Full stack repointing
  • Worn or open joints
  • Neat, consistent finish
  • Longer chimney life
Get a free quote

06

Chimney Crown Repairs

The crown sheds water away from the flues at the very top of the chimney. Cracks here let rain straight into the stack.

We repair or re-lay the crown with durable materials and re-bed loose pots while we are up there.

  • Cracked crowns
  • Failed flaunching
  • Re-bedding pots
  • Capping and cowls
Get a free quote

07

Full Chimney Stack Removal

Sometimes keeping a stack is no longer practical: it is structurally tired, costly to maintain, or in the way of a roof renovation.

Removal is more than taking down brickwork. We assess how the chimney is built and what it supports, protect the roof and the property, dismantle it carefully, then make the roof good.

  • Structural assessment first
  • Roof and property protected
  • Careful dismantling
  • Roof made good
Get a free quote

08

Chimney Leak Repairs

A leaking chimney can come from flashing, mortar, the crown or the brickwork itself. The stain on the ceiling rarely tells you which.

We trace the water back to where it gets in, fix the cause, and protect the roof and property from further moisture damage.

  • Leak tracing
  • Flashing and cement work
  • Damp patches and staining
  • Urgent call-outs
