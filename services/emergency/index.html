Good to know

Questions we get asked.

Something else on your mind? Ring 085 173 8320 and ask.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. Inspections and quotes are free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. All of our chimney work is guaranteed.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. We are available 24/7 for emergencies such as loose brickwork or water coming in. Ring 085 173 8320.

Can my chimney be repaired, or does it need rebuilding?

It depends on its condition. We assess the stack first and only recommend a rebuild when it is unstable or too far gone to repair properly.

What areas do you cover?

Dublin City and the surrounding areas, including Rathmines, Tallaght, Drumcondra, Clontarf, Ranelagh, Ballsbridge and Dundrum.
